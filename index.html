Research

  • Microscopic optical potential for deformed nuclei
    Universidad de Chile, Mar 2025 to present. Supervisor: Dr. Hugo Arellano

    I am building an optical potential for scattering from deformed nuclei from the effective interaction (g-matrix) of Brueckner–Bethe–Goldstone theory. The potential uses a multipole expansion of the nuclear density and a functional expansion of the effective interaction. I am now implementing it numerically to compute differential cross sections and compare them with data.

  • Granular matter: charge measurement experiment
    Universidad de Chile, Dec 2025 to Jan 2026. Supervisor: Dr. Nicolás Mujica

    I contributed to the design of a shorter vacuum-tower experiment that measures particle charge from trajectories in a uniform electric field. Simulations of charged-particle trajectories helped show that a fixed camera over a shorter range could replace the falling camera, a major source of error. I also modeled Lorentzian distributions to quantify measurement uncertainty.
